FICTIONAL WRITING CHECKS

Draft: 'received imad will work on it'
Expected: 'Received, thank you. Imad will work on it.'
Do not add a deadline, explanation or second version.

Draft: 'we are checking no root cause yet'
Expected: 'We’re investigating, but the root cause has not been identified yet.'
Do not say the issue is resolved.

Draft: 'can you confirm if circuit stable now'
Expected: 'Could you confirm whether the circuit is stable now?'
Do not answer the question as a troubleshooting request.
